# KEYSTROKE

### Find your rhythm. Build your speed.

**A focused typing practice app with live stats, personal progress, and a keyboard sound studio.**

<p align="center">
  <a href="https://keystroke-ks.vercel.app/"><strong>Open KEYSTROKE →</strong></a>
</p>

Practice in your browser: watch your words per minute and accuracy update as you type, then review your results and see how your speed changes over time.

## What you can do

- **Practice your way:** choose a typing test mode and track live WPM, accuracy, and progress.
- **Explore themes:** browse a large collection of color themes or create and save your own.
- **Review your progress:** see recent results, personal bests, and typing statistics on this device.
- **Make it yours:** personalize your typing experience, sound settings, and optional mini game.
- **Try keyboard sounds:** explore synthesized switch profiles in the Sound Studio.

## Get started

Visit **[keystroke-ks.vercel.app](https://keystroke-ks.vercel.app/)** and choose **Test** to begin. Your browser saves your local settings and typing history on your device.

## A note about rankings

Results and history are stored locally in your browser. The Leaderboard view shows your local results; shared public rankings are not connected.
